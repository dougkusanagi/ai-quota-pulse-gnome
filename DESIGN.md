# Indicador GNOME
Mode: Operate. Quiet, solid surfaces: native panel button between Activities and the clock; a light/dark popup with 1px hairlines. No blur; motion limited to size, opacity and rotation. Matches windows-notch.
Panel indicator: Claude left, Codex right; 14/20px progress ring per account (initials inside when a provider has several), tabular percent beside a single account. Fits inside the top bar; never overlaps the clock or browser tabs.
Popup (380px, opened by click only): provider sections of 5h / weekly rows (label, 5px bar, percent, reset), footer with last update, refresh and settings. Settings and the account editor are sub-screens inside the native popup with a back button; Esc or an outside click returns to details.
Colour: neutral ink/surface tokens in light and dark; semantic bar tone by remaining quota (green > 40%, amber > 15%, red); accent follows the system accent for primary actions, switch, focus and selection.
Type: GNOME theme font; 16/13/12/11px scale, tabular figures for every number. Secondary text keeps >= 4.5:1.
Errors name the cause and recovery action; missing data never becomes a fabricated quota.
